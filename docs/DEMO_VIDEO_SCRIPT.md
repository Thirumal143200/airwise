# AIRWISE: Urban Air Quality Intelligence & Family Safety Assistant
## Official Demo Video Script (3–4 Minutes)

**Target Video Duration**: 3 minutes 30 seconds  
**Target Delivery**: YouTube Unlisted / Google Drive Public Link  
**Tone**: Authoritative, empathetic, technically rigorous, and user-centric.

---

### [00:00 - 00:30] Scene 1: The Problem — Moving Beyond Passive AQI Scoreboards

**Visual**:
- Browser opens on AirWise showing Delhi with an AQI of 385 (Severe).
- Cursor points to the prominent Severe Smog Alert banner and the CPCB AQI hero card.

**Narration**:
> *"Every winter across the Indo-Gangetic plains and global metropolitan centers, millions of families wake up to toxic smog. Traditional weather apps display a scary red or maroon number—AQI 385—and stop there. They leave parents and commuters paralyzed with unanswered questions: Can my child play outside at 3 PM? Should I crack a window or keep the home sealed? If I cycle to work to be green, am I poisoning my lungs?*
>
> *Introducing **AirWise**—Urban Air Quality Intelligence. AirWise does not merely report pollution; it provides actionable, personalized daily routines, indoor safety practices, and transparent environmental impact calculations to help families make cleaner, safer decisions."*

**Visual**:

- Click "Tune Household Profile".

- Click "Apply & Update Routine". The 4 cards instantly re-render.

**Narration**:
> *"This is the core of AirWise: the Personalized Air-Safe Routine Engine. 
>
> Notice how our advice adapts: for a family with children during a severe smog alert, the morning card cancels all outdoor jogs and school assemblies due to dangerous thermal inversion trapping fine particles in the breathing zone. 
>
> It doesn't just say 'stay inside'; every recommendation provides 6 essential dimensions: What to do, Why it is suggested scientifically, When it is most useful, A practical indoor alternative, The likely health benefit, and Important limitations like indoor CO2 buildup."*

### [02:00 - 02:35] Scene 4: Indoor Air Quality & Sustainable Alternatives

**Visual**:
- Click "Indoor Air Guide" tab.
- Toggle filters: "Zero-Cost (Behavioral)", "Low-Cost ($20–$60 DIY)", and "Certified HEPA Appliances".
- Zoom in on the Corsi-Rosenthal Box walkthrough, the CARB Ozone Generator Warning, and the Houseplants reality check.
- Switch to "Cleaner Choices" tab to show the Exposure-Emission Tradeoff cards.

**Narration**:
> *"Not every household can afford a $400 air purifier. AirWise provides tiered guidance: from zero-cost damp mopping—which prevents toxic dust resuspension—to open-source $35 DIY Corsi-Rosenthal filtration boxes. We also provide life-saving public health warnings against ozone-generating ionizers.
>
> In our Cleaner Choices section, AirWise embodies our core engineering principle: **The Exposure-Emission Balance**. We never advise an individual to cycle in severe smog merely to reduce emissions; inhaling 40 liters of toxic air per minute causes severe lung injury. We prioritize enclosed, filtered electric transit."*

**Visual**:

- Point out the instant calculation ($4.59\,kg\,CO_2e/\text{day}$ cut, annual trees offset, and the mathematical formula box showing DEFRA 2024 emission factors).

- Show the structured JSON response card with recommendation, rationale, action steps, confidence rating, and the transparent engine badge.

**Narration**:
> *"In the Transparent Impact Calculator, there are no 'black-box' carbon numbers. Users see the exact arithmetic, DEFRA 2024 and ARAI emission factors, uncertainty margins, and an explicit disclaimer distinguishing long-term climate CO2 mitigation from immediate roadside particulate exposure.
>
> Finally, our GenAI Copilot answers personalized family safety questions. Grounded strictly in verified environmental measurements, it returns structured, validated guidance. And if the external AI service is ever offline, AirWise automatically switches to its deterministic expert rules engine with full badge transparency."*
