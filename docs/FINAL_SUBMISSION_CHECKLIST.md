### 2. Description of Changes Made in Deployed Version

*(Copy-paste ready for official submission form)*:

> **AirWise — Urban Air Quality Intelligence & Family Safety Assistant** is an original full-stack environmental assistant built from scratch for PromptWars Challenge 6. 
>
> **Key Architecture & Capabilities**:
> 1. **Live Air Quality & Dual Standards**: Real-time atmospheric chemistry integration (Open-Meteo & Copernicus CAMS atmospheric reanalysis) supporting both Indian CPCB NAAQI and US EPA AQI standards for 6 pollutants ($PM_{2.5}, PM_{10}, NO_2, SO_2, CO, O_3$) with WHO 2021 24-hour health comparisons.
> 2. **Personalized 4-Slot Daily Routine Planner**: Dynamically generates morning, midday, evening, and bedtime recommendations based on thermal inversion dynamics and user profile (children, elderly, commute habits, asthma, budget). Every card includes what to do, scientific reason, optimal window, practical alternative, benefit, and limitations.
> 3. **Tiered Indoor Air Quality Assistant**: Evidence-based indoor protocols spanning zero-cost (damp mopping vs dry sweeping), low-cost ($35 DIY Corsi-Rosenthal boxes), and certified True HEPA filtration, with explicit public health warnings against harmful ozone-generating ionizers.
> 4. **Sustainable Alternatives Engine**: Resolves the crucial exposure-emission paradox (protecting users from hazardous advice like unmasked jogging or cycling in severe smog merely to reduce emissions).
> 5. **Transparent Impact Calculator**: Fully transparent mathematical formula modeling avoided vehicle kilometers, fuel saved, avoided $CO_2e$, and EPA mature tree absorption equivalents with DEFRA 2024 and ARAI emission factors.
> 6. **GenAI Copilot with Deterministic Rules Fallback**: Server-side Google Gemini orchestration (`gemini-1.5-flash`) with structured JSON schema output, bounded timeouts (8s), rate limiting, and an automated deterministic rules engine fallback ensuring zero downtime.
> 7. **Engineering Rigor**: Strict TypeScript, 24 passing Vitest tests across 6 test suites, 0 ESLint warnings, 0 TypeScript errors, WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility (multi-modal sensory risk encoding without color reliance), and sliding-window rate limiting.
